# AI assistance disclosure

BLACKBOX was built with AI-assisted software-development tools during The
Agent Harness Hackathon. Codex through T3 Code helped research the event and
TrueForge interfaces, challenge the design, draft and revise code, write tests
and documentation, diagnose failures, and review changes. Qodo also reviewed
implementation pull requests.

Davi Barbosa chose the problem and product scope, directed the architecture
and security boundaries, supplied and controlled the external accounts,
reviewed the generated work, resolved review findings, and ran the deterministic
and real-provider acceptance checks. Security conclusions are computed by the
Evidence Ledger and covered by executable checks; they are not accepted from an
AI assistant's narrative or from the Victim Agent's self-report.

No production secret or third-party private data is part of the Attack
Scenario. The demonstrated Canary Secret, Support Ticket, internal documents,
and External Sink are synthetic and controlled by the project.
